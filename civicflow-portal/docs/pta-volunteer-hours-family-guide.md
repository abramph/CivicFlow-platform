# Unestra for PTA — Volunteer Hour Requirements & Buyout: Family Guide

This describes what a parent/guardian sees if their PTA has turned on Volunteer Hour Requirements. It's fully responsive — usable from a phone, tablet, or desktop browser, since the native mobile app doesn't have this yet (see the note at the end).

## Where to find it

**My PTA** page → **Volunteer Requirement** card. If your PTA hasn't turned this on, you won't see this card at all — nothing changes for you.

## What you'll see

- Your family's required hours for the current period, and how much of that is already covered — split out into verified (approved) hours, hours pending an officer's approval, purchased hours, and any waived/credited hours.
- Whether your family is currently marked exempt.
- Any outstanding balance if a remaining-hours assessment has been posted to your account, with a **Pay assessment** button.

## Buying out some or all of your hours (if your PTA allows it)

If your PTA has turned on buyouts, you'll see options to:

1. **Complete the hours yourself** (the default — no payment involved).
2. **Purchase all required hours** (if your PTA allows a full buyout).
3. **Purchase part of your hours** — enter how many hours you want to buy, in whatever increment your PTA allows (e.g. whole hours or half hours).

**Get a quote** shows you the exact cost, computed by the server — never a price you can influence from your browser. Selecting an option and confirming it **records your choice — it is not a payment yet**. Hours are only credited to your account once payment is actually confirmed. If you chose a paid option, you'll then see a **Pay now** button that takes you to a secure Stripe checkout. Depending on how your PTA configured this period, the price you pay is either the rate active right when you start checkout, or the rate you were quoted when you made your choice, held for you until that pricing option closes — your PTA can tell you which applies.

Refunds, if needed, follow your PTA's stated refund policy — talk to your PTA officers.

## Paying a remaining-hours assessment

If your family didn't complete or buy out all required hours by the period's deadline, your PTA may post an assessment — a charge for the hours that weren't fulfilled. You'll see it on your Volunteer Requirement card with a **Pay assessment** button, which takes you to secure checkout the same way a buyout does.

## Reporting a missing or incorrect volunteer record

If you volunteered for something and it's not showing up, or a recorded shift looks wrong, use **Report a missing or incorrect volunteer record** right on the card. Describe the shift or activity (date, event, who volunteered) — an officer will review it. This doesn't automatically change your hours; it flags it for a human to look at.

## Downloading your own volunteer report

If your PTA has Reports turned on, you'll see **Download my volunteer report** — a formatted Excel summary of your family's hours, buyouts, and assessments for the current period, for your own records. This only ever contains your own family's data, never anyone else's, and never an officer's internal notes about your assignment.

## A note on the mobile app

The Unestra mobile app does not yet have Volunteer Hour Requirements/Buyout screens — this is a deliberate first-phase decision so the version of the app currently being reviewed by Apple/Google isn't disrupted (see `docs/pta-volunteer-hours-mobile-phase3-spec.md` for what's planned for a later release). Until then, everything above is available through your phone's web browser at the same portal address you already use.
